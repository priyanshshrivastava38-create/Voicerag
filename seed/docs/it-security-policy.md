# IT Security Policy

## Scope

This document is synthetic test data created for the VIH VoiceRAG pilot.

This policy applies to all employees, contractors and devices that access VIH Metaverse systems.

## Passwords and authentication

Passwords must be at least 14 characters long. Multi-factor authentication with an authenticator app is mandatory for all accounts; SMS codes are not permitted.

## Devices

All laptops must use full-disk encryption. USB mass-storage devices are blocked on company laptops.

A lost or stolen device must be reported to the IT service desk within one hour of discovering the loss.

## Data classification

Information is classified into four levels: Public, Internal, Confidential and Restricted. Restricted data may only be stored in approved systems with access logging.

## Phishing and incidents

Suspected phishing emails must be reported using the Report Phish button in the mail client. Do not forward suspicious emails to colleagues.

Access rights to production systems are reviewed every quarter by system owners.
