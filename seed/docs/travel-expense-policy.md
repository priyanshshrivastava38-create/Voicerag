# Travel and Expense Policy

## Scope

This document is synthetic test data created for the VIH VoiceRAG pilot.

This policy covers business travel and reimbursable expenses.

## Flights

Economy class is used for flights under 6 hours. Premium economy is allowed for flights of 6 hours or longer. Business class requires CFO approval.

## Per diem and hotels

The domestic per diem is 3,500 rupees per day and the international per diem is 75 US dollars per day.

Hotel stays in metro cities are capped at 9,000 rupees per night.

## Claims

Expense claims must be submitted within 30 days of the expense. Itemised receipts are required for any expense above 500 rupees.
