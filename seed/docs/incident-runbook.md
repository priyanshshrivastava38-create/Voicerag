# Incident Response Runbook

## Severity levels

This document is synthetic test data created for the VIH VoiceRAG pilot.

SEV1 means a full outage or a security breach affecting customers. SEV2 means a major feature is degraded for many customers. SEV3 covers minor issues with a workaround.

## Response targets

For a SEV1 incident the on-call engineer must acknowledge the page within 5 minutes, and status updates are posted every 30 minutes until resolution. For SEV2, the response target is 30 minutes.

## Roles

Every SEV1 and SEV2 incident has an incident commander who coordinates the response and a communications lead who updates stakeholders.

## Postmortems

A blameless postmortem is required for every SEV1 and SEV2 incident and must be published within 5 business days.
