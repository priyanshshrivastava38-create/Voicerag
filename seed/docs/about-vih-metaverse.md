# About VIH Metaverse

## Who we are

ViH Metaverse Pvt. Ltd. builds a state-of-the-art customer experience ecosystem that connects enterprises with their customers at every touchpoint, delivering unparalleled experiences from first contact to lasting loyalty.

The ecosystem leverages AI, Blockchain and the Metaverse to redefine how enterprises understand, engage and serve their customers.

## Product overview

VIH Metaverse offers five AI products: ViH Messenger, ViH Shruti, ViH Buddhi, ViH Smriti and ViH Prana. They work independently and better together, covering chat, voice, email, documents and meetings for 360 degree omnichannel customer coverage.

## ViH Messenger

ViH Messenger is an enterprise-grade IP2IP messaging SDK that connects a brand to its customers through intelligent, always-on conversations. Features include direct IP2IP enterprise messaging infrastructure, embedded AI chatbot conversations, real-time customer engagement at scale, and an SDK-first design for seamless enterprise integration.

## ViH Shruti

ViH Shruti is an AI voicebot: intelligent voice AI that handles customer interactions over voice channels with full conversational context. Features include natural language voice interactions, cross-channel context retention, multilingual voice support, and seamless handoff to human agents.

## ViH Buddhi

ViH Buddhi is an insight engine that mines support emails and documents to surface quality signals, turning unstructured data into actionable intelligence. Features include automated email and document analysis, user behaviour pattern extraction, support quality scoring and reporting, and trend detection across communication streams.

## ViH Smriti

ViH Smriti is an AI meeting companion that joins, records and analyses meetings. Features include automated recording and transcription, multi-speaker identification and attribution, cross-meeting context and target tracking, and actionable key notes and follow-ups.

## ViH Prana

ViH Prana is the unified intelligence layer, the engine powering omnichannel intelligence. Every touchpoint, including chats, voice calls, emails and meetings, feeds into Prana. Prana stitches them into a single intelligence graph, generates automated triggers, and gives the enterprise omnichannel analytics to understand both individual cases and the systemic patterns behind them.

## How the products work together

Example: a telecom customer raises a billing dispute in chat, then calls, then emails. ViH Messenger routes the chat to the AI chatbot and flags escalation. ViH Shruti answers the call with context from the chat and flags churn risk. ViH Buddhi extracts invoice data from the email and detects a pattern of billing failures. ViH Smriti records the internal review meeting and generates action items. ViH Prana links everything so the support team can resolve the issue proactively.
