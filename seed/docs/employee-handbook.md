# Employee Handbook

## About this handbook

This document is synthetic test data created for the VIH VoiceRAG pilot.

The handbook summarises how we work at VIH Metaverse. Where a dedicated policy exists, the dedicated policy takes precedence.

## Working hours

Core collaboration hours are 10:00 to 16:00 India Standard Time, Monday to Friday. Outside core hours employees may arrange their schedule flexibly with their team.

Meetings should not be scheduled outside core hours unless all attendees agree.

## Hybrid and remote work

Employees may work remotely up to three days per week with their manager's approval. Fully remote arrangements require approval from the department head and HR.

Remote employees receive a one-time home office stipend of 600 US dollars, renewable every two years.

## Probation and notice

The probation period for new employees is six months. After probation, the notice period for resignation is sixty days for all roles.

## Office location

The headquarters is at Tower B, Level 9, in the VIH Innovation Park. Visitors must be registered at reception by their host at least one day in advance.
